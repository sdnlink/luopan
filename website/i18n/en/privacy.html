Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026 · Developer: Beijing Changxian Technology Co., Ltd. (北京长线科技有限公司) · support@sdnlink.cn

Core commitment: The app primarily computes on-device and has no developer backend receiving personal profiles, sensor readings, camera frames or floor plans. Apple handles purchases. Some preferences may sync through Apple iCloud; system backups and files you choose to save or share are handled as described below.

Introduction

Welcome to TianJi YanXiang (“the App”), developed and operated by Beijing Changxian Technology Co., Ltd. (北京长线科技有限公司) (“we”). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App and what rights you have.

1. Information and Permissions

The App asks for the following permissions only when you use the relevant feature.

Personal and family profiles; report customization

The new iOS app does not collect birth, gender or life-gua profiles or provide personal predictions or related room allocation. Previous profiles are retained for users and can be deleted free in Settings → Privacy and data; deletion also requests cleanup of accessible old iCloud profile copies.

Projects may contain room names, dimensions, plans, openings, scanned models and exports. Data is primarily stored on device. You initiate sharing and backups; check home and personal details before sharing.

Motion and Direction Sensors

Motion sensors support AR tracking and view stabilization on device. The new app has no compass feature and does not request location permission.

Camera

Use: Only for live preview and spatial alignment in the AR survey feature. Storage: Frames are processed locally in real time, without automatic recording or upload to developer servers. Survey snapshots you choose to capture, save or share may include camera images and are written to local archives, Photos or your selected sharing destination.

LiDAR / RoomPlan Room Scanning

Use: On supported devices, the App uses Apple’s ARKit and RoomPlan frameworks to scan rooms and produce spatial geometry. Storage: Scan data is processed on-device. If you export or share spatial files, they may contain building structure, orientation, or location information; whether to share them is your decision.

Saved surveys may also contain room geometry, doors and windows, directions, annotation points and ARWorldMap data used to restore spatial positioning. These archives are stored locally; manage exported, backed-up or shared copies where they are stored.

Photos

Projects may contain room names, dimensions, plans, openings, scanned models and exports. Data is primarily stored on device. You initiate sharing and backups; check home and personal details before sharing.

Local Notifications (Optional)

The new app does not schedule solar-term or auspicious-date reminders. On upgrade it removes this app’s old pending and delivered local notifications.

In-App Purchases (StoreKit)

Apple StoreKit verifies purchases and restorations. Entitlements and legacy credits are stored on device with integrity checks; verification keys are in Keychain. We do not receive card details. The new interface sells annual and lifetime access only. Legacy-credit migration requires original records and keys; contact support if verification fails.

2. Third-Party SDKs and Data Sharing

The app has no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, does not collect IDFA, does not track across apps or websites, and does not sell or rent your data. Payments, iCloud and system backups handled by Apple, and recipients you choose when sharing, process data within the scope described here and under the applicable service rules.

3. Data Retention and Security

Floor-plan drafts, survey archives, reports and settings are stored in the iOS app sandbox or the App Group shared with the widget, depending on the feature. Purchase rights and credits are stored in integrity-checked local UserDefaults; the verification key is stored in Keychain. Not all archives are stored in Keychain. Data remains until deleted. After app removal, treatment of the sandbox, shared container and Keychain follows iOS behavior; removal does not automatically delete Apple cloud copies, system backups, Photos or copies held by others.

Apple iCloud, system backups and migration

The new app does not sync compass preferences, reminders or personal prediction data. Apple may retain older cloud copies. Deleting previous profiles requests removal of accessible corresponding copies, which may sync after reconnecting. Manage system backups and exported files separately.

Privacy API purposes

UserDefaults stores settings for this app and its App Group; file timestamps manage local survey files; system uptime measures AR refresh intervals. There are no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, no cross-app tracking and no upload of profiles, plans or camera frames to developer servers.

4. Your Rights and Choices

  • Revoke camera or photo access in system settings. Delete previous profiles free in app settings and projects in the project list. A new floorplan replaces the current draft. Delete backups, exports and shared copies separately. Deleting the app does not cancel Apple subscriptions.

  • Questions and requests: For questions about data handling or to exercise rights available under applicable law, email support@sdnlink.cn.
  • Subscriptions: Subscriptions, refunds, and billing are handled by Apple; manage them in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Revoke location, camera, Photos and notification permissions in iOS Settings; only the related features are affected. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription; manage it in Apple ID settings. Apple backups, iCloud and shared files are handled by Apple or recipients under their own rules. Contact support@sdnlink.cn for privacy or deletion requests.

5. Children’s Privacy

The app is not directed to independent use by children under 13. Parents or guardians may enter a child’s name or label, birth information and gender for family room-allocation references. Obtain appropriate permission and enter only necessary information. These profiles follow the local family-data rules and can be deleted through the free family data management screen. Contact support@sdnlink.cn for assistance.
