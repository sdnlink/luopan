5. Privacy and User Content

Camera, location, motion-sensor, and photo permissions are requested only when the relevant feature is used. The current version processes scans and analysis primarily on-device and does not provide its own upload API. Images and spatial files saved or shared voluntarily may contain location, orientation, building structure, or other sensitive information. Review them before sharing and confirm that you have the right to use their contents. See our Privacy Policy.

Personal and family profiles: Names or labels, relationships, birth dates or years, and gender that you voluntarily enter are used for life-gua and room-allocation references. Obtain the person’s or guardian’s permission before entering another person’s or a child’s information. Profiles are stored on the device and in the App Group shared by this app and its widget. Family profiles are no longer automatically uploaded to or restored from iCloud.

Preferences and Apple iCloud: Compass-lock preferences and action lists may sync through Apple’s iCloud key-value store when supported by the distributed app and system settings. This update stops automatic cloud sync of personal family profiles. Deleting a profile also requests removal of accessible legacy iCloud profile copies; offline deletion may finish syncing after reconnection.

Retention and deletion: Data remains until you delete it. With or without a paid membership, open Settings → Family data management to delete individual members or clear all owner and family profiles and disable personal analysis. This removes local, shared-container and accessible legacy iCloud profile copies. Previously exported, shared or separately archived copies must be deleted where they are stored.
