Core capabilities

Compass and calendars

View magnetic and true north, declination, 24 mountains, traditional divisions, solar terms and apparent solar time. Includes direction locking, cultural direction references and an almanac widget.

AR spatial surveys

Use the camera, LiDAR and RoomPlan on supported iPhone or iPad models to record interiors and review candidate annotations based on traditional form rules.

RoomPlan and property archives

Import or create multiple floor-plan archives. Save, load and compare center points, room relationships and 3D layouts.

Family room allocation and chart exploration

Explore Ba Zhai life gua, Xuan Kong, annual and monthly Purple White, Qi Men and traditional date selection for the next 30 days, with plain explanations and textual references. Personal analysis and room allocation require annual or lifetime membership.

Cultural scrolls and daily direction cards

Combine current measurements, owner life gua, floor plans and analysis into a scroll report, or create daily cards to save and share.

Eight languages

Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, Indonesian and Thai.

Device requirements

Requires iOS 16.0 or later. AR, LiDAR and RoomPlan depend on hardware; other devices can use supported compass, floor-plan and cultural-analysis features.

Intended use

Measurements and recognition depend on device, environment and operation; VR models depend on entered dimensions. Results support records, study and cultural reference. Verify important measurements on site.
